🥞 Classic Homemade Pancakes

Fluffy, golden pancakes that are easy to make and perfect for breakfast or brunch.

Recipe Details

  • Prep Time: 10 minutes
  • Cook Time: 15 minutes
  • Servings: 4 people
  • Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. In another bowl, whisk the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix gently.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour batter into the pan and cook until bubbles form.
  6. Flip and cook until both sides are golden brown.

Tips & Notes

For extra flavor, add vanilla extract or serve with fresh fruits and honey.

Nutrition Facts (Optional)

Approx. 220 calories per serving.
